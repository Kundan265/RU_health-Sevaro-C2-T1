02

Our mechanism: an event-driven loop

Each new event flows through the same eight stages. Only one stage needs a person.

⏱
Event time, not arrival timeLate data goes where it belongs. Duplicates count once.
⇄
Two independent clocksA doctor's activity and each consult's stage are tracked separately.
▣
Hard rules before rankingCredentials, rest and hours are gates. No rule is ever bent.
◆
Protect scarce credentialsDon't spend the last doctor who can cover another hospital.
⚖
Lowest safe decision-makerSystem, coordinator or lead: each fix goes to the right owner.
✋
One human gateNo message reaches an off-shift doctor without approval.

Why Sevaro needs this

Sevaro's mentor named the root problem: surges concentrated in a few hospital systems, where credentials limit who can help. That problem grows as the network grows.

The most material changes are coverage forecasting, actual upload handling, active credential preservation, and complete handoff timing. The forecast includes the full 120-minute endpoint, physician boundaries and hospital rule boundaries. The reproduced SYN-008 gap at 08:07 in the 06:27 scenario is now detected. Upload identity uses bytes rather than filename and size; changed same-name/same-size files process, repeated events remain idempotent, and missing columns or invalid timestamps are rejected before state changes. Paused interactions hold time; explicit advancement and future uploads move the scenario forward.

Clinical Lead is now the default approver. A proposal retains its required approver even if the configuration changes. Sending follows approval, and acceptance changes scheduled coverage for the named hospitals. Staffing context is separate from patient-consult handoffs. Real consult packets preserve prior owner, original stage entry, remaining deadline, prior physicians and timestamped routing events. Pending, overdue and simulated acknowledged states use the scenario clock; acknowledging late does not reset the consult deadline.

The active ranking retains credentials, routing availability, different-owner and more-than-30-minute remaining-shift gates. Eligible doctors sort by other at-risk hospitals losing their last eligible alternative, then open-consult load, shift consult count and stable physician ID. It uses present eligibility as a scarcity heuristic; it is not a global allocation optimizer or guarantee of future usable capacity.

The controlled supplied-credential scenario chooses PHY-003 for CON-2200 rather than the less-loaded PHY-001. Choosing PHY-003 removes the last eligible alternative at one other at-risk hospital; PHY-001 would remove it at two. This is **2 → 1**, not elimination of scarcity. The event fixture deliberately changes activity and adds two synthetic consults while retaining the original roster and credentials. It establishes why this rule can beat load-only selection in that case, not general superiority over Sevaro.

The approved PHY-009 offer changes two projected gap hospitals to zero at the reference clock, with online time 18:35. This is scheduled credentialed coverage, not an immediate fix for CON-2105 or a clinical outcome. The existing 20-call routing counterfactual allocates 10 calls to an unrestricted joiner versus 5 with gap-hospital restrictions and a seeded count. This remains a deterministic model, not an external routing integration.
